GB DEVCART · mechanical · DMG Game Pak

Does it fit a Game Boy cartridge shell?

Every board below is checked against a parametric DMG shell: 57 × 65 × 7.8 mm, with a 53.2 mm cavity and 3.3 mm of room on the label side. The original Game Boy, Game Boy Color, Analogue Pocket and ModRetro Chromatic all take that one cartridge outline. The shell is modelled from community CAD replicas and the board outline from gekkio's GB-CART256K-A. Nintendo has published no drawing, so test-fit one print first.

Through the thickness

A section across the cart, with the back face at 0. The shell's front ribs clamp the board's component face at 2.5 mm, so a thicker board pushes its back face into the back wall. Parts get 3.3 mm above that face.

Checks

Results from mech/fit_check.py, which parses each .kicad_pcb (Edge.Cuts, NPTH holes, finger pads, courtyards and part heights). It exits non-zero on any fail.

Getting r1.1 and r2.1 to fit

  1. Start from the OEM outline in mech/out/dmg_reference.kicad_pcb. It is 51.4 × 61.0 with a 49.2 mm finger tongue and a 4.0 × 2.6 lock notch at the top right.
  2. Cut holes for the shell: Ø7.2 at (25.7, 15.35) for the screw tube and Ø2.3 at (25.7, 40.35) for the post, both measured from the board's bottom-left corner.
  3. Swap J1 for gbdev:GB_Cart_Edge_32_DMG. Its fingers start 1.5 mm above the edge, and GND/VCC sit 1.0 mm above it so they make contact first. Order ENIG or hard gold.
  4. Order 0.8 or 1.0 mm FR-4. A 1.6 mm board pushes into the back wall.
  5. Put every part on the label side, inside the dashed zone, no taller than 3.3 mm. Nothing goes in the bottom 11.4 mm, which sits inside the console's connector.
  6. r2: put USB-C J2 on the top edge, its mouth flush with the board edge, and print shell_front_usb. At 3.26 mm the receptacle leaves 0.04 mm under a 3.3 mm wall, so the front half has a 0.8 mm pocket over it. The lock notch rules out the top-right corner.
  7. r2 needs 4 layers at 1.0 mm. JLCPCB offers 4-layer boards at 0.8 or 1.0 mm.

Which consoles

ConsoleFits?Note
Game Boy (DMG), Pocket, LightyesThe power-switch tab slides into the top-right notch, so keep the notch or the DMG won't switch on.
Game Boy ColoryesSame outline. The notch is ignored.
ModRetro ChromaticyesModRetro's own carts are standard DMG-size shells that also run on a DMG, a GBC and an Analogue Pocket. No slot-depth spec is published.
Analogue PocketyesNative GB slot. Some flash carts have trouble on newer revisions; that is an electrical issue, not a mechanical one.
GBA / GBA SPsticks outRuns in GB mode at 5 V and protrudes from the slot. The board's 5 V input copes with that.
